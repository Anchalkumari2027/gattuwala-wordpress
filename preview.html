OUR STORY

Helping turn biomass potential into real-world production.

Gattuwala Energy Solutions supports biomass fuel production through pellet machinery, processing equipment and plant setup guidance. We work with businesses looking to make better use of agricultural residues, sawdust and other suitable biomass feedstocks.

Our focus is on understanding project requirements, recommending practical equipment and supporting customers through the stages of planning and operation.

Our mission

To advance biomass processing technology and help expand access to efficient, sustainable fuel production solutions.

Our vision

To contribute to a more resource-efficient economy by helping industry convert suitable biomass residues into useful products.

GATTUWALA EQUIPMENT

Vertical Ring Die Pellet Machine GTW7 Series

Engineered specifically for demanding biomass feedstocks including bagasse, sawdust, paddy straw, and groundnut shells.

Key Specifications

  • Capacity: 1.5 – 3.0 Tons / Hour per unit
  • Main Motor: 90kW - 132kW Siemens / ABB high-efficiency motor
  • Die Type: Double layer alloy steel vertical ring die
  • Lubrication: Automatic circulating oil cooling system

The GTW7 Series incorporates high-precision gear driving, independent automatic lubrication, and an intelligent control cabinet with overload protection.
